Drywall repair is the single most-requested job we get, and also the one homeowners have the hardest time pricing ahead of time. A nail hole and a water-damaged section of ceiling both technically fall under "drywall repair," but they're not remotely the same job — or the same price.

If you're a Huntsville homeowner staring at a hole, a crack, or a water stain and wondering what it'll cost to make it disappear, here's a transparent breakdown of what actually drives the number.

## The Three Things That Set the Price

### 1. Size and Type of Damage
This is the biggest factor, and it's not a straight line — a bigger hole isn't just "more of the same small hole," it's a different category of work.

*   **Small holes and dings (under 6 inches):** Doorknob holes, nail pops, and small dents. These are quick patches — mesh tape or a self-adhesive patch, two coats of mud, sand, done. The fastest and cheapest repair category.
*   **Medium holes (6 inches to 2 feet):** Usually from furniture moves, a doorstop failure, or a removed fixture. These need a cut-in patch tied into the surrounding studs or blocking, which takes more time to blend invisibly than a small hole does.
*   **Large sections and full-sheet replacement:** Water damage, mold remediation, or a wall that's been opened up for plumbing or electrical work. This is effectively a mini installation — hanging new sheet, taping every seam, and building up multiple coats of joint compound before it's paint-ready.

### 2. Texture Matching
Huntsville homes span everything from 1970s heavy knockdown to smooth modern builds, and matching the existing texture is where a repair either disappears or turns into an obvious patch on the wall.

*   **Smooth/Level 5 finish:** The easiest to match — it's just a matter of feathering the mud out far enough that there's no visible ridge.
*   **Orange peel and knockdown texture:** Requires a hopper gun and the right technique to lay down texture that matches the surrounding wall's pattern and density. Get this wrong and the patch is visible from across the room even after paint.
*   **Popcorn ceilings:** The hardest to match well, since older popcorn texture has often yellowed or the exact spray pattern is no longer sold. We'll tell you honestly if a perfect blend isn't realistic before we start.

### 3. What Caused the Damage
A hole from moving a couch is a same-visit patch. A stain on the ceiling means we have to find and confirm the leak is actually fixed before we can safely close the wall back up — otherwise you're paying to patch the same spot again in six months. Any repair connected to water damage or mold gets an inspection step added before the drywall work starts.

## What a Typical Job Looks Like

Most calls we get fall into one of two buckets:

*   **A handful of small-to-medium holes in one visit** — nail pops from settling, a doorknob hole, a dented corner. We knock these out together in a single trip since the setup and cleanup time is shared across all of them.
*   **One larger repair from a specific incident** — a plumbing leak, a removed cabinet, storm damage to a ceiling. These take a return visit for a second or third coat of mud, since each coat needs to dry before the next one goes on and gets sanded.

Either way, we'll tell you upfront whether it's a same-day patch or a multi-visit job before we start, so there's no surprise about the timeline.
